## Ready-to-use listing copy

### Short tagline

Free browser-based Matcha Filter remover for photos and videos.

### One-sentence description

Reduce the Matcha Filter's green cast, added grain and flattened contrast in photos or videos, with local browser processing and no upload or account.

### Full description

Remove Matcha Filter is a free browser-based correction tool for photos and video clips. It measures the visible colour balance, reduces a green cast and added grain, restores usable contrast and lets the user compare the source with the adjusted result before export. Media stays on the user's device: there is no upload, account or credit requirement. The tool is explicit about its limits—it can improve visible colour and texture, but it cannot reconstruct original pixels that an effect replaced or discarded.

## Claims that are safe to use

- Free to use with no account or credits.
- Photos and videos are processed locally in the browser.
- Photo export is PNG, with a 4096px long-edge processing ceiling.
- Video correction is applied frame by frame, up to 1920px on the long edge.
- Supported video audio is preserved; unsafe silent fallback is blocked.
- The tool reduces visible colour, contrast and texture changes.

Do not claim that the tool reveals hidden content, restores an untouched original, uses generative AI, downloads TikTok URLs, or is affiliated with TikTok or Instagram.
