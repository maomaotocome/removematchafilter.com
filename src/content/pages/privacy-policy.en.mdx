## The short version

**Your photos and videos are never uploaded.** The tool has no server-side
media processing. When you choose a file, your browser decodes it locally, your
GPU renders the adjusted version onto a canvas in the same tab, and the export
is written directly to your device's downloads folder. Your media never reaches
our servers, so we have no copy to store, inspect, share or train anything on.

## How the tool handles your media

When you pick or drag in a file:

1. The browser creates an object URL — a reference only this document can
   resolve. It is not a network address and cannot be fetched by anyone else.
2. A downscaled copy of one frame is measured in memory to derive the colour
   correction. It is discarded when you load another file.
3. Rendering happens through WebGL on your own hardware.
4. Export encodes from that canvas and hands the file to your browser's normal
   download mechanism.

Every step runs inside the tab. Closing or reloading the page releases the
object URL and discards the decoded frames; nothing persists.

## What we do collect

**Server logs.** Requesting pages from this site produces ordinary web server
logs: IP address, timestamp, requested path, referrer and user agent. These are
kept briefly for security and debugging, and they record page requests only —
never file contents, filenames or slider values.

**Local browser storage.** A small number of preferences live on your device,
such as your light/dark theme choice and the language cookie used by the
internationalisation layer. They stay on your device.

**Analytics, if enabled.** If privacy-respecting aggregate analytics are active
on this site, they record page views and referrers — not your media and not your
adjustments. No analytics provider receives your files, because your files are
never transmitted anywhere.

## Accounts and payments

This site is built on a platform that includes optional account, subscription
and support features. The matcha filter tool does not use them: you do not need
to sign in, and there is nothing to pay for. If you never create an account, we
hold no account data about you. Should those features be offered later, this
policy will be updated before they go live.

## Third parties

We do not sell or share your personal information. We do not send your media to
any third party, including AI or cloud-processing services, because the tool
never transmits your media at all. Ordinary infrastructure providers — hosting
and CDN — process page requests on our behalf under their own terms.

## Children

The tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export
or delete personal information we hold about you. Because the tool works without
an account and never receives your media, there is usually very little to act
on. Send requests to the address below.
