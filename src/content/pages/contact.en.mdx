## What to include for a bug report

The tool runs entirely inside your browser, so almost every problem comes down
to the combination of browser and file. Sending these four things lets us
reproduce it without ever needing your media:

1. Your browser and version, and your operating system.
2. Whether you were in Photo or Video mode.
3. The file type and rough size — for example "42 MB MOV from an iPhone".
4. The exact error text the tool showed, if any.

Please do **not** attach the photo or video itself. We have no pipeline for
receiving customer media and would rather not start one; the reproduction
details above are enough.

## Known limits worth checking first

- **Video export unavailable.** Your browser lacks `MediaRecorder` canvas
  capture. Adjusting and previewing still work. Recent Chrome, Edge and Firefox
  can export.
- **A MOV file will not open.** HEVC-encoded video generally cannot be decoded
  outside Safari. Re-export as H.264 MP4 or WebM.
- **Exported video has no sound.** Expected. Canvas recording captures picture
  only; mux the original audio back with a video editor if you need it.
- **"This browser cannot start WebGL."** Hardware acceleration is disabled or
  unavailable. Turning it on in your browser settings usually fixes it.
