## What the service is

Remove Matcha Filter is a free, browser-based tool that reduces the appearance

## What the tool does and does not do

The tool **reduces and adjusts** the filter look — the green colour cast, the
flattened contrast, the grain overlay, the smeared micro-detail.

It **does not recover the original pixels.** A filter discards image
information before you ever receive the file, and no software can read back
what is not there. We make no claim that the tool restores an original image,
reveals hidden or removed content, or produces a forensically accurate
reconstruction. Results vary with the source file, and some images will not
improve much.

Do not rely on output from this tool as evidence, as a factual record, or for
any purpose where an accurate original matters.
